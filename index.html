Welcome To LogicLoom TechFest 2026

Where Logic Shapes Innovation.


LogicLoom TechFest 2026 is a celebration of technology,innovation, and logical thinking. Join us for exciting competitions, creative challenges, and platform to showcase your tech skills.

Join us and be part of s tech fest that celebrates logic, learning and Innovation.

About LogicLoom TechFest


  • LogicLoom TechFest is an annual technical festival organized by the Department of BCA(Bechelor of Computer Application).

  • The fest is designed to peovide students with a platform to explore technology, enhance logical thinking, and develope problem-solving skills through a variety of technical and non-technical events.

  • LogicLoom focuses on nurturing innovation, creativity and technical excellence.

  • It encourages participants to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world challenges and gain practical exposure in areas such as programming, web development, and analytical reasoning.

  • The fest is open to students who are passionate about technology and eager to learn, compete, and collaborate in a challenging yet engaging environment.

Events & Competitions

1.Problem Solving

Test your logical thinking and analytical skills through challenging problems.

About: Problem Solving is a technical event that tests participants logical thinking and analytical skills through carefully designed reasoning-based questions. The focus is on understanding problems and applying correct logic within a limited time.

Rules & Guidelines:

  • Individual participation Only.
  • Time limit: 30 minutes
  • Use of electronic devices is strictly prohibited.
  • Any form of malpractice will result in disqualification.

Evaluation Criteria

Participants will be evaluated based on:

  • Accuracy of answers
  • Logical reasoning and approach
  • Time management
  • Clarity of solutions

Eligibility

  • Open to all undergraduate students.
  • Students from any discipline can participate.
  • Basic knowledge of logical reasoning is sufficient.
  • No coding experience is required.

2.Innovation Idea Presentation

Test your logical thinking and analytical skills through challenging problems.

About: Innovation Idea Presentation is an event where participants present a creative technical idea that addresses a real-world problem. The focus is on originality, clarity of thought, and practical relevance of the proposed solution.

Rules & Guidelines:

  • Guidelines & Rules
  • Individual participation only.
  • Participants must present one original technical idea.
  • Presentation duration: 3–5 minutes.
  • Ideas may be presented using a PPT or verbal explanation.
  • The idea must be related to technology or digital solutions.
  • Plagiarism or copied ideas will lead to disqualification.
  • Judges’ decision will be final.

Evaluation Criteria

Participants will be evaluated based on:

  • Innovation and originality
  • Clarity of explanation
  • Practical feasibility
  • Presentation skills

Eligibility

  • Open to all undergraduate students.
  • Participants from any branch may apply.
  • No working model is required.
  • Basic understanding of technology is sufficient.

3.Debugging Contest

Test your logical thinking and analytical skills through challenging problems.

About: Debugging Contest is a technical event where participants identify and correct errors in given code snippets. The event tests logical thinking, attention to detail, and understanding of basic programming concepts.

Rules & Guidelines:

  • Individual participation
  • Participants will be provided with faulty code snippets.
  • Programming language will be announced before the event.
  • Event duration: 30 minutes.
  • Corrected code must follow proper syntax and logic.

Evaluation Criteria

Participants will be evaluated based on:

  • Number of errors correctly identified
  • Accuracy of fixes
  • Logical approach
  • Time management

Eligibility

  • Open to all undergraduate students.
  • Participants should have basic knowledge of programming concepts.
  • No working model is required.
  • Suitable for beginners and intermediate-level students.

4.Speed Coding

Test your logical thinking and analytical skills through challenging problems.

About: Speed Coding is a competitive programming event where participants solve coding problems within a limited time. The event emphasizes speed, logic, and code efficiency.

Rules & Guidelines:

  • Individual participation
  • Time limit: 45 minutes
  • Participants will be given a set of programming problems.
  • Allowed programming languages will be announced before the event.
  • Solutions must produce correct output for given test cases.
  • Use of unfair means will lead to disqualification.

Evaluation Criteria

Participants will be evaluated based on:

  • Number of problems solved
  • Correctness of solutions
  • Time taken to submit solutions
  • Code efficiency and logic

Eligibility

  • Open to all undergraduate students.
  • Basic knowledge of programming is required.
  • Suitable for beginners and intermediate-level students.

5.Tech Quiz

Test your logical thinking and analytical skills through challenging problems.

About: Tech Quiz is a knowledge-based event designed to test participants’ understanding of computer fundamentals and basic programming concepts. The quiz focuses on accuracy, speed, and logical thinking.

Rules & Guidelines:

Evaluation Criteria

Participants will be evaluated based on:

  • Correctness of solutions

Eligibility

  • Open to all undergraduate students.
  • Basic knowledge of programming is required.
  • Suitable for beginners and intermediate-level students.

6.Web Design

Test your logical thinking and analytical skills through challenging problems.

About: Web Design is a creative technical event where participants design a responsive and visually appealing website interface. The event focuses on layout, creativity, usability, and basic frontend skills.

Rules & Guidelines:

  • Individual participation
  • Participants must design a frontend-only web page.

Test your logical thinking and analytical skills through challenging problems.

Rules & Guidelines:

  • The test consists of algorithm-based and logic-oriented questions.

Evaluation Criteria

Eligibility

  • Open to all undergraduate students.

  • No advanced programming experience is required.
